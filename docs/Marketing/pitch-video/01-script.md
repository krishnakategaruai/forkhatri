# ForKhatri: pitch and marketing video

**Version 4 (2026-10-02).**

**The purpose of this stage:** to let our Samaj know that something real is being built, for a purpose, by one of us. The film shows the vision and mission, and what already works. Investment, ownership, legal structure and volunteer details come later and are kept out of this film.

## Three films

| Film | For | Length |
|---|---|---|
| **A. The Samaj film** | Samaj leaders and the people who will run ForKhatri | about 5 min |
| **B. The member film** | every family, forwarded on WhatsApp | about 2 min 30 s |
| **C. Reels** | WhatsApp status, Instagram, chapter groups | 30–45 s each |

Narration: English first, with Telugu and Hindi versions after. One calm Indian-English male voice (Prabhat), which suits the brother's story.

## Rules that keep it easy to watch

1. One idea per frame.
2. At most six words of on-screen text, in big type.
3. At most three app screens per module. Hold each for 3 seconds or more, zoomed to the part that matters.
4. Icons, not lists.
5. Slow narration, about 110 words a minute, with a pause after each key line.
6. Faces before screens.
7. Captions are always on.

**The one line: "Our people. Verified. In every city."**

## Words we use and words we avoid

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| protected, flagged, screened | "cannot be saved", "scams are caught", "blocked" |
| checked by our Samaj | "verified" without explanation |
| being built, planned, coming | "live" or "working today" for payments, finance or the Trust |
| our people, trusted, united | words that rank one community above others, and the Parashurama episode |
| everyday words: "a good match must suit both families" | technical terms, model names, formulas |
