FORKHATRI
Complete High-Level Business Requirements Document
Document Type: High-Level Business Requirements
Product: ForKhatri
Purpose: Foundation for Product Strategy, SDLC Planning and Module-Level Requirements
1. Product Definition
ForKhatri shall be a modern, trusted and technology-driven community ecosystem designed to
connect people through shared heritage, culture, values, traditions and collective responsibility.
ForKhatri shall enable members to access:
Trusted people and networks
Opportunities
Businesses and professionals
Community events
Matrimonial services
Professional counsel
Local information
Payments and member benefits
Financial services
AI-powered assistance
ForKhatri shall begin as a community-focused platform and establish a scalable model for broader
community and societal development.
2. Vision
To build a trusted, progressive and united community where shared heritage becomes a
foundation for individual opportunity, collective prosperity and generational progress.
3. Mission
To connect people through shared culture, values and traditions, and empower them with trusted
networks, knowledge, opportunities and support to build better lives and a stronger community.

11. Trust and Accountability Requirements
Trust shall be a platform-level capability shared across ForKhatri.
The platform shall support a common trust foundation while allowing every business module to
maintain its own verification requirements.
11.1 Common Identity
ForKhatri shall provide a unified member identity.
A member should not require a completely separate identity for each ForKhatri service.
11.2 Module-Specific Verification

1825. AI Action Principle
AI should not have unrestricted authority over member accounts.
The preferred interaction model shall be:
Understand → Recommend → Authorize → Execute → Record → Confirm
Risk-sensitive actions shall require appropriate explicit authorization.
AI activity shall remain subject to:
Permissions
Privacy
Security
Accountability
Auditability
Safety controls
26. AI as Personal Community Assistant
The long-term ForKhatri experience should move toward:
One intelligent assistant capable of understanding the member and coordinating
authorized ForKhatri services on their behalf.
The AI should understand appropriate member context without unnecessarily exposing personal
information across modules.
