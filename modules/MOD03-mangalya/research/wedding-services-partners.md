# Wedding services and partner vendors for Mangalya

Research date: 1 October 2026. Every claim has a source link. Where a figure comes from a vendor forum, analyst blog or other secondary source rather than the company itself, it is marked **(secondary)**. Treat those figures as rough ranges, not facts.

**The question:** once two families accept each other's request, Mangalya wants to suggest services near them that fit their stage: cafes for the first meeting, function halls, decorators, photographers, vehicles and caterers. Later, once it has many users, it wants to agree partner discounts. How do other platforms earn money from vendors, and how should Mangalya do this honestly?

---

## 1. How the existing platforms earn money from vendors

### 1.1 The four ways to charge vendors

| Model | How it works | Who uses it |
|---|---|---|
| **Listing subscription** (monthly, quarterly or yearly fee for a profile, with higher tiers for better placement) | The vendor pays whether or not anyone books. Pricing depends on city and category. | WedMeGood, The Knot, WeddingWire, VenueLook |
| **Lead fee or credits** (the vendor pays for each enquiry, or spends credits to reply) | Pay-as-you-go. It fits small vendors but pushes them to chase volume. | Zola (free listing, credits to reply), WeddingWire premium tiers (lead credits bundled with visibility) |
| **Commission on booking** (a percentage of the booking value) | Only paid when a booking happens. The platform has to track the booking, which is hard if the family pays the vendor directly. | Matrimony.com Mandap/Bazaar, BookEventz, Weddingz.in (managed venues), planners at 10–15% |
| **Featured or sponsored placement** (an add-on, sold on top of any of the above) | Top slots, a "Spotlight" position, or category takeovers. | Almost all of them |

### 1.2 What each platform does

**Matrimony.com group (BharatMatrimony, Mandap.com, WeddingBazaar, Matrimony Photography, ShaadiSaga)**
- Its wedding brands are Mandap (venue finder), Matrimony Photography, MatrimonyBazaar (jewellery, catering, honeymoon and similar) and MatrimonyDirectory. The company says it works "on a commission basis with the vendors on each of these portals" ([exchange4media, IPO coverage](https://www.exchange4media.com/marketing-news/matrimony.com-goes-publicplans-to-take-business-to-the-next-level-70487.html)).
- Mandap launched in Tamil Nadu, Hyderabad/Secunderabad, Kochi and Bengaluru with about 2,000 mandaps and halls ([MediaNama 2017](https://www.medianama.com/2017/10/223-matrimony-com-expands-marriage-venue-booking-service-states/)). It now claims more than 17,000 venues in over 100 cities ([matrimony.com/services/mandap](https://www.matrimony.com/services/mandap), [Global Dating Insights](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/news/matrimony-com-expands-wedding-venue-finder-platform-across-india/)). Mandap and WeddingBazaar together list more than one lakh vendors ([Matrimony.com NSE disclosures](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/MATRIMONY_05022025212456_NSEMediarelease05Feb25.pdf)).
- Matrimony.com bought ShaadiSaga (a wedding vendor marketplace) in 2021 for about ₹11 crore ([exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/digital-news/matrimonycom-to-acquire-a-100-stake-in-shaadisaga-114115.html)). **WeddingWire did not buy ShaadiSaga.** WeddingWire India is a separate business that launched in Gurgaon in 2017 ([The Knot Worldwide press release](https://www.theknotww.com/press-releases/weddingwire-international-expansion)).
- **The key lesson:** despite its size, revenue from "marriage and other services" is tiny. It was ₹4.6 crore a quarter in Q2 FY18 ([MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2017/11/223-matrimony-com/)) and has fallen to about ₹1 crore a quarter in Q2 FY26 ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=538448)). Matchmaking subscriptions are the business. Wedding services have not turned into large revenue, even for the market leader, and Mangalya should not plan on vendor money early.

**Shaadi.com**
- Paid memberships make up most of its revenue. One secondary source says Shaadi.com earns a 20–30% commission on wedding-service deals worth ₹50,000 or more ([boldsystems summary](https://v5.boldsystems.org/?p=39928), **(secondary, unverified; low confidence)**). We found no first-party Shaadi.com source for a vendor commission rate.

**WedMeGood (India's largest independent vendor marketplace)**
- It charges vendors a subscription, quarterly or half-yearly. Early on, 80% of its revenue came from subscriptions and 20% from brand tie-ups. Priority listing costs more ([Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/adit-mathai/before-you-say-i-do-2724), [YourStory 2016](https://yourstory.com/2016/03/indian-wedding-industry)).
- Revenue was ₹44.8 crore in FY25, up 57.9% from ₹28.4 crore ([Inc42 company page](https://inc42.com/company/wedmegood/), [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/?p=32831)).
- Reported premium-listing prices are about ₹8,000–25,000 a month, depending on city and category ([planning.wedding](https://planning.wedding/weddingwire-listing-cost), **(secondary)**).
- **Its terms** ([wedmegood.com/terms](https://www.wedmegood.com/terms)):
  - Profiles are created by vendor sign-up, by WedMeGood's sales team, from user submissions, or from public business information. The last two are "unclaimed" profiles that the vendor can claim later.
  - Fake or paid reviews are banned. When a review is disputed, WedMeGood may ask for proof and remove it. Reviews must be written within 2 years of the event.
  - Ranking may include "commercial arrangements where applicable and disclosed".
  - WedMeGood says it does **not** verify every credential, and does not guarantee quality or prices.

**WeddingWire India**
- It sells premium packages that bundle lead credits with visibility. Reported prices are about ₹12,000–30,000 a month in competitive metro categories. "Spotlight" positions cost about 2.5 times the base rate ([planning.wedding](https://planning.wedding/weddingwire-listing-cost), **(secondary)**).

**The Knot (USA)**
- Pure advertising: vendors pay for a storefront, and there is **no commission and no per-lead fee**.
- Reported prices ([planning.wedding](https://planning.wedding/is-the-knot-worth-it-for-vendors), [Curate](https://curate.co/blog/the-knot-advertising-cost/), **(secondary)**):
  - $50–150 a month in rural markets
  - $500–1,200 or more a month in big metros
  - premium placements of $6,000–12,000 a year
  - usually 12-month contracts
- Storefront features are described by The Knot itself ([WeddingPro](https://pros.weddingpro.com/?p=12440)).

**Zola (USA)**
- Vendor listings are free and Zola has promised to keep them free. Vendors pay in credits to connect with couples, which is a pay-per-lead model. It reports more than 19 million leads sent ([Funktastic Chats interview with Zola](https://funktasticchats.buzzsprout.com/1543315/episodes/15290610-kate-furst-from-zola-chats-how-their-new-vendor-marketplace-connects-couples-and-wedding-pros), [FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/how-does-zola-make-money/)).
- Its main income is registry and retail margin, not vendors.

**Venue aggregators in India**
- **BookEventz:** takes a commission after each booking, and also sells premium listings ([Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/bookeventz), [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/bookeventz)).
- **VenueMonk and VenueLook:** charge a quarterly or yearly marketing fee instead ([YourStory: VenueMonk](https://yourstory.com/2016/11/venuemonk), [YourStory: VenueLook](https://yourstory.com/2016/06/venuelook)).
- **Weddingz.in:** is free for families. It acts as the "sales and marketing partner" for 750+ managed venues, which give it their best rates because of the volume it brings ([BW Disrupt](https://bwdisrupt.com/article/weddingzin-an-online-marketplace-for-wedding-services-97127)).

**Typical percentages in India**
- Wedding planners usually take **10–15% from vendors** ([HitchedIndia](https://hitchedindia.com/wedding-planner-commission-hidden-costs/)).
- The Wedding Company charges families about **4% of the budget**, plus a platform fee from suppliers based on volume ([YourStory 2025](https://yourstory.com/2025/07/startup-the-wedding-company-planning-indias-middle-class)).
- Venue commissions are not published. From the models above, 5–15% of the booking value is a realistic range to negotiate, but this is **our inference, not a published figure**.

### 1.3 How discounts are negotiated and shown
- **Negotiation is based on volume.** Weddingz.in and The Wedding Company get "best rates" or volume fees in exchange for the bookings they bring. Deals come *after* the platform can show demand.
- **How deals appear on screen:** an "Offer" or "Exclusive" badge on the vendor card, and an offer line on the profile, for example "10% off decor for [platform] users" or a free pre-wedding shoot. The platform usually gives a code or reference to quote to the vendor.
- **How reviews and verification work across the market:** unclaimed profiles can be claimed, the platform may ask for proof when a review is disputed, and reviews expire after a set time. Almost every platform says in its terms that it does not guarantee vendor quality.

## 2. The wedding journey after the families agree, and what is needed at each stage

**Telugu sequence** ([Wikipedia: Telugu Hindu wedding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telugu_Hindu_wedding), [WeddingBazaar blog](https://www.weddingbazaar.com/blog/telugu-wedding-rituals), [Aza Fashions](https://www.azafashions.com/blog/guide-to-pellikoduku-pellikuthuru-ceremonies/), [Desievite](https://desievite.com/invitation-blogs/telgu-pre-wedding-rituals)):

| Stage (Telugu / Hindi) | Rough timing | What happens | Services that matter |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Pelli choopulu / rishta dekhna** (first meeting) | Days to weeks after the families accept each other | The groom's family visits the bride's home, or they meet at a neutral place | Quiet **family-friendly vegetarian restaurants and cafes** (private rooms, parking), sweet shops (for gifts to take along) |
| **2. Horoscope match and talks** | 1–4 weeks | The families match horoscopes and agree terms | Priest or astrologer (Mangalya already has horoscope features) |
| **3. Nischitartham / roka, sagai** (engagement) | 1–3 months after the first meeting | The muhurtham date is fixed from both horoscopes | Small **function hall** or hotel banquet room (50–200 guests), **caterer**, **photographer**, simple **decor**, jewellers (rings, gold), clothing |
| **4. Booking the muhurtham date and venue** | **Hyderabad hotel banquets for good dates are booked at least 3–4 months ahead** ([Deccan Chronicle](https://deccanchronicle.com/nation/in-other-news/050318/wedding-mania-hits-hyderabad-as-muhurat-is-good.html)). The general advice is 7–12 months for popular venues in peak season ([Manyavar](https://manyavar.com/en-in/blog-indian-wedding-planning-timeline-simple-steps.html), [GRT Jewels](https://www.grtjewels.com/grtbrides/10-mistakes-to-avoid-when-booking-your-indian-wedding-venue/)) | Busy muhurtham dates fill halls quickly | **Function halls, convention centres, kalyana mandapams, temple halls** (the first thing to book) |
| **5. Vendor bookings** | 3–6 months before the wedding | | **Caterer** (often tied to the hall), **decorator** (mandapam and stage), **photo and video**, **mehendi artist**, makeup, **pandit or purohit**, nadaswaram or band, printed invitations, return gifts |
| **6. Pellikuturu / Pellikoduku** (turmeric and oil ritual, like haldi) and mehendi | 1–2 days before | Held at each family's home | Mehendi artist, a small decorator, photographer |
| **7. Wedding day: mangala snanam, then the muhurtham** (often close to midnight in Telugu tradition, per [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telugu_Hindu_wedding)) | Wedding day | | **Vehicle rental** (car for the groom's procession, buses or tempo travellers for guests), hall, caterer, decor, photography |
| **8. After the wedding: vratam, reception** | Days after | | Reception venue, caterer, photographer, travel for the honeymoon |

**What this means for Mangalya:**
- The **only service that is useful immediately** after acceptance is the **first-meeting place**.
- **Halls** are the most urgent booking once a date is fixed, because of the muhurtham rush.
- Everything else only matters after the engagement.
- Mangalya should suggest services in stages, driven by the stage the couple has reached (accepted, meeting planned, engaged, date fixed). It should not show a full vendor catalogue on the day two families accept.

## 3. Free and legal place data before any partners exist

### 3.1 OpenStreetMap tags to query (Overpass `nwr`, meaning nodes, ways and relations)

| Service | Exact OSM tags |
|---|---|
| First-meeting cafes and restaurants | `amenity=cafe`, `amenity=restaurant`. Refine with `diet:vegetarian=yes\|only` and `cuisine=indian\|south_indian\|regional` |
| Function halls, banquet and marriage halls | `amenity=events_venue` (the wiki defines it as "a place that provides facilities for events such as banquets, weddings etc.", status de facto, and notes it suits South Asian banquet halls: [wiki](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Tag:amenity=events_venue), [help thread](https://help.openstreetmap.org/questions/45898/how-to-tag-a-hall-that-is-for-hire-for-banquets-weddings-conventions-exhibitions)). Also `amenity=community_centre` and `amenity=conference_centre`. **Many Indian halls are tagged loosely, so also match on the name:** `name~"function hall\|convention\|banquet\|kalyana mandap\|marriage hall",i` |
| Caterers | `craft=caterer` ([wiki](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Tag:craft%3Dcaterer)) |
| Photographers | `craft=photographer` ("can be hired to photograph events (wedding…)") and `shop=photo` ([wiki](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/Tag:shop=photo)) |
| Decorators | No specific tag exists. Use `shop=party` ([wiki](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Tag:shop=party)) and `shop=florist` |
| Vehicle rental | `amenity=car_rental` ([wiki](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Tag:amenity=car_rental)) |
| Useful extras | `shop=jewelry`, `shop=clothes`, `shop=tailor`, `shop=beauty` (mehendi and makeup), `shop=confectionery` (sweets), `amenity=place_of_worship` with `religion=hindu` (temple weddings) |

**What OSM actually has for Hyderabad.** We ran a count on overpass-api.de on 1 October 2026, over the Hyderabad administrative area:

| Tag | Places found |
|---|---|
| `amenity=restaurant` | 942 |
| `amenity=cafe` | 297 |
| `amenity=community_centre` | 116 |
| `amenity=events_venue` | 69 |
| `amenity=conference_centre` | 38 |

Hyderabad has thousands of real restaurants and hundreds of function halls, so **OSM covers cafes and restaurants reasonably well but halls thinly.** It will have **almost nothing useful on caterers, decorators or photographers**. (A second count for those tags timed out on both public servers because they were busy.)

**Conclusion:** OSM is good enough for the first-meeting cafe and restaurant suggestion and for a starter list of halls. For the other categories Mangalya needs partner or vendor-submitted data.

### 3.2 OSM licence and attribution
- The data is under the **ODbL**. Show **"© OpenStreetMap contributors"**, linked to https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright, wherever OSM-derived places appear ([OSMF attribution guidelines, adopted 25 June 2021](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Attribution), [OSM wiki](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Attribution)).
- If Mangalya **adds to and redistributes** an OSM-derived database, that database must stay under ODbL ("share-alike").
- Mangalya's own partner data is safe if it is kept in a **separate table** and only shown alongside OSM data. In ODbL terms, a "collective database" does not have to be shared under ODbL.

### 3.3 Overpass usage policy
- Public server limits are about **10,000 queries and 1 GB per day**, and the manual says "setting up an app for more than just OSM mappers and relying on the public instances as backend" is misuse ([Overpass manual](https://dev.overpass-api.de/overpass-doc/en/preface/commons.html)).
- **For a regular service the limit is 10,000 divided by 100, so about 100 queries and 10 MB a day. That total counts every user of the app together.** Other rules ([OSM wiki: Overpass API](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Overpass_API)):
  - send a User-Agent that identifies the app
  - no parallel queries
  - cache the results
  - commercial use should self-host or use a paid service
- **So Mangalya must never call Overpass from the browser for each user.** Pull a Telangana or Andhra Pradesh extract ahead of time, either as a nightly or weekly batch job or from a Geofabrik `.osm.pbf` download. Load it into Postgres/PostGIS and answer "near me" questions from Mangalya's own database.

### 3.4 Google Places at a glance
([Places API policies](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/policies), [Service terms](https://cloud.google.com/maps-platform/terms/maps-service-terms))
- It is a paid API, and Google's data is better than OSM for Indian halls and caterers.
- **Only `place_id` may be stored indefinitely.** Nothing else may be pre-fetched, cached or stored, apart from latitude and longitude, which some APIs allow for 30 days.
- If Places data is shown without a Google map, the **Google Maps logo or text must be shown**.
- Reviews and photos must credit their author and link back to Google Maps (`googleMapsUri`).
- **So Google Places cannot be the base of Mangalya's own partner directory.** At most, use it live to fill in a partner's details on screen, and store only the `place_id`.

## 4. Trust, privacy and the law

**Location privacy between families**
- Never show one family's home, area or live location to the other family, or to any vendor.
- To find places "near them", use a **midpoint chosen by the family** or an area they pick themselves (for example "near Kukatpally"), not GPS.
- Vendors should never get a family's contact details until the family taps "Contact" or "Share my number". Mangalya should show the vendor's phone number to the family, not the other way round.
- The **DPDP Rules 2025** were notified on 13 November 2025. The consent, notice and breach rules take full effect 18 months later, in **about May 2027**. They require a standalone, plain consent notice for each purpose ([Hogan Lovells](https://www.hoganlovells.com/en/publications/indias-digital-personal-data-protection-act-2023-brought-into-force-), [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/12/26/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-key-highlights/)). Sharing a lead with a vendor is a separate purpose and needs its own consent.

**Showing paid placement honestly**
- **Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020, Rule 5(3)(f):** a marketplace must publish, in plain language, the main factors that decide how sellers are ranked and how much each counts ([MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2020/07/223-consumer-protection-ecommerce-rules-2020/), [Khaitan](https://khaitanco.com/sites/default/files/2020-08/Stricter%20Regulations%20on%20E-Commerce%20%E2%80%93%20The%20Consumer%20Protection%20(E-Commerce)%20Rules,%202020.pdf)).
- **The E-Commerce Amendment Rules 2026 were notified on 9 September 2026 and apply from 1 January 2027** ([AZB Partners](https://www.azbpartners.com/?p=89613), [Khaitan Ergo](https://www.khaitanco.com/sites/default/files/2026-09/Ergo_E-Commerce%20Amendment%20Rules_18%20September%202026.pdf)). They add:
  - "sponsored listings… distinctly identified with clear and prominent disclosures"
  - ranking factors listed **in descending order** of importance, with "ranking" now also covering how prominent a seller is
  - each seller's name and geographical address shown
  - a ban on manipulating search results
  - yearly dark-pattern audits
  - National Consumer Helpline integration becomes mandatory
- **CCPA Dark Patterns Guidelines, 30 November 2023:** "disguised advertisement" is a banned dark pattern ([Trilegal](https://trilegal.com/knowledge_repository/guidelines-for-prevention-and-regulation-of-dark-patterns-2023/), [Vidhi](https://vidhilegalpolicy.in/blog/from-trickery-to-transparency/)). A paid partner must never look like a neutral "recommended for you" suggestion.
- **ASCI influencer guidelines:** an ad must be "obviously distinguishable" from organic content, using approved labels only (#ad, #sponsored, #collab, #promo, #partnership). The label must be upfront and prominent, **in the language of the content** ([Legal500](https://legal500.com/developments/?p=28469), [exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/announcements-news/asci-drafts-guidelines-for-influence-advertising-on-digital-media-111084.html)). Mangalya needs accurate Hindi and Telugu versions of "Sponsored" and "Partner offer".
- **BIS IS 19000:2022 on online reviews** (voluntary; [Business Today](https://businesstoday.in/latest/trends/story/fake-online-reviews-bis-releases-new-standard-for-online-consumer-reviews-356195-2022-12-13)):
  - no bought reviews, and no reviews written by people employed for that purpose
  - paid or incentivised reviews must be disclosed
  - there must be a moderation policy
- **Partner discounts should not change the order of results.** If they do, that must be disclosed as a ranking factor.

## 5. Recommended phased plan for Mangalya

### v1 (now): useful, no money involved, fully honest
1. **"Plan your first meeting" card**, shown only when both families have accepted.
   - It suggests 5–8 **vegetarian-friendly, family-suitable restaurants and cafes** near an area both families agree on, from the OSM extract, with "© OpenStreetMap contributors".
   - Families can share a chosen place into their Mangalya conversation.
   - No home locations are used.
2. **Stage-aware checklist** (first meeting, engagement, date fixed, wedding) that shows the relevant categories in the order in section 2.
   - Halls come first as soon as a date is fixed, with the note "good muhurtham dates fill 3–12 months ahead".
   - OSM halls are shown as "Places on the map".
3. **No paid placement and no "partner" badge yet.** A static page explains the order: "Sorted by distance from the area you chose. No business pays to appear here."
4. **Families can add a vendor** they used and write a short experience note. Only families with a completed engagement or wedding stage can write reviews. This starts Mangalya's own directory without needing Google data.
5. **Store OSM data in a separate table**, refreshed weekly from a Geofabrik extract. Never call Overpass live.

### v2 (with a few thousand active families in Hyderabad): verified partners
- Vendors claim or submit a profile. Mangalya checks phone OTP, a GSTIN or trade licence where the business has one, a photo of the premises, and a sample of real work.
- Families only get leads when they tap "Ask for a quote". The family's number is shared only with explicit consent for that purpose.
- Revenue options, in order of preference:
  - a **flat monthly listing fee** (tier-2 pricing, well below WedMeGood's ₹8–25k)
  - or a **pay-per-accepted-lead** fee
  - Avoid commission until bookings can be tracked.

### v3 (at scale): negotiated partner offers
- Offers negotiated against volume, for example "Mangalya families get 10% off decor" or "a free engagement shoot".
- Each offer has a code the family quotes to the vendor, which also tracks the booking and makes a later commission possible (5–15% range).
- **Sponsored slots** are clearly labelled and limited to one or two per list, and are **never** placed in the first-meeting suggestion.
- Publish the ranking factors in descending order (required from 1 January 2027).

### Partner data model (the fields)

```
service_place            -- one row per real business (OSM, family-added or partner)
  id, source (osm | family_added | partner), osm_type+osm_id (nullable), google_place_id (nullable)
  name, name_te, name_hi, category (cafe_restaurant | function_hall | caterer | photographer |
      decorator | vehicle_rental | mehendi_makeup | priest | jeweller | clothing | sweets | temple_hall)
  stages[] (first_meeting | engagement | date_fixed | wedding | post_wedding)
  lat, lng, area_name, city, full_address           -- business address, public (required by the 2026 rules)
  phone, whatsapp, website, email
  veg_only (bool), capacity_min/max (halls), price_band (₹ / ₹₹ / ₹₹₹), languages_spoken[]
  verification_status (unverified | claimed | verified), verified_at, verified_by, verification_evidence_ref
  gstin (nullable), business_legal_name
  attribution (e.g. "© OpenStreetMap contributors"), last_refreshed_at, is_active

partner_agreement        -- only for businesses that signed up
  id, service_place_id, plan (free | listing_monthly | pay_per_lead | commission)
  fee_amount, commission_pct, starts_on, ends_on, signed_by, status

partner_offer            -- a deal, always shown as a "Partner offer"
  id, service_place_id, title/title_te/title_hi, terms (plain words), discount_type (percent | flat | freebie)
  discount_value, min_booking_value, valid_from, valid_to, redemption_code_pattern, stages[]
  is_sponsored_placement (bool)   -- true means the business paid for position; label shown as "Sponsored"

lead                     -- created only when a family taps "Ask for a quote"
  id, service_place_id, candidate_profile_id, requested_by_member_id, consent_id, shared_fields[]
  event_date (optional), guest_count (optional), status (sent | responded | booked | closed), offer_id (nullable)

place_review             -- only from families at the matching stage
  id, service_place_id, author_member_id, stage_at_review, rating, text, language
  event_date, incentivised (bool, disclosed), moderation_status, created_at   -- expires 2 years after the event
```

### How to show deals honestly (screen rules)
- Every paid thing has a visible label in the member's language: **"Sponsored"** (the business paid for position) or **"Partner offer"** (a discount agreed with Mangalya). Never a vague label like "Recommended".
- The offer's terms, validity and "how to use" are shown on the card itself, not hidden behind extra taps.
- Default order is **by distance from the area the family chose**. A "How this list is ordered" link lists the ranking factors in descending order.
- The first-meeting suggestions are **never sponsored**. They should feel like advice from a trusted elder, not an ad.
- Businesses from OSM show the OSM credit. Verified partners show "Verified by Mangalya on <date>" and say what was checked.
- Families' locations, phone numbers and profile details never reach a vendor without a tap and a consent line saying exactly what is shared.
