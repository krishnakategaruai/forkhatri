## P0: must fix

### P0-1 FUNCTIONAL: a parent cannot use the matchmaker for his child
- **Screen / seat:** `/me/matchmaker` and `/me/matchmaker?setup=1`, family seat (Vikram, "Viewing Kavya's search"), 390.
- **What is wrong:** both URLs open "Step 1 of 4 · What is your name? · This is how you will appear to families". That is the flow for creating Vikram's own candidate profile, not Kavya's matchmaker. Home hides the matchmaker card whenever the seat acts for someone (`app/page.tsx`: `{!forId && profile && (… /me/matchmaker)}`). Discover's "Tell us who you're looking for" entry is missing in the family seat as well.
- **Why it matters:** in South India, 45–50% of accounts are made by parents, and 78% of Tamil Brahmin profiles are parent-run (evidence research §3.1). The owner's rules say parents act for the candidate and that Home carries "their matchmaker". At the moment the most AI-first feature in the product is closed to the family that uses the product most.
- **Fix:** scope the brief to the candidate, not to the account. `/me/matchmaker?for=<candidateId>` reads and writes Kavya's brief with the parent as editor. Home shows "Kavya's matchmaker" in the family seat. Discover shows the entry card with `titleFor`. `?setup=1` must never send a family seat into own-profile onboarding.
- Shots: `shots\mm-setup-family-390.png` (same result for `/me/matchmaker` without `setup`).

### P0-2 FUNCTIONAL / privacy: the sealed name appears in the parent-seat biodata

The name is sealed in one place and printed in the next. Name plus employer is enough to find him on LinkedIn.

### P0-4: "Strong match" stands next to a horoscope result the family would reject

## P1

### P1-1: the candidate sees almost nothing of the profile; the parent sees everything
- **Profile, candidate seat (390/1280).** Asha sees 3 chips (city, job, education) and "Why you might work". Vikram, looking at the same person, sees 27 biodata rows (height, family, diet, income, birth details…). The owner's model: before acceptance, the groom decides what is visible. Role changes only the *style*, and after connection the candidate sees "formal information *and* the prompts".
- **Fix:** render the fields the groom published in the candidate lens too, below the photo story ("The basics" in a compact 2-column fact grid, as Hinge shows height, job and hometown in a row of chips). Only prompts, answers and the extra photos stay sealed. Birth time and place must be visible to everyone by owner decision (mangaly-birth-details-public-horoscope-filter), and the candidate lens does not show them at present.

### P1-2: the parent-seat biodata reads like a database dump
- **Profile, family seat, 1280.** 27 rows in one flat list, under form-question labels: "A few lines about you", "Height (cm)", "Willing to relocate?", "How sure is the time?". Labels are about 12px muted grey. On desktop it is a single column 1,100px wide with a full-width red button.
- **Why:** the owner asked for a "conventional detailed profile" with a real desktop multi-column layout. Parents in their 50s–60s scan by section, which is what the printed biodata trained them to do (research §3.2).
- **Fix:** group into About · Education & career · Family · Horoscope & birth · Lifestyle. Use reader-facing labels ("About him", "Height", "Open to relocating", "Birth time: exact, from the birth record"). Show 175 cm as "5′9″ (175 cm)". Use two columns at 1024px and above: biodata on the left, and a sticky Compatibility plus actions panel on the right. Labels at least 14px, values 16–17px.

### P1-3: plans hide GST until checkout (drip pricing)
- **/plans, 390.** "₹2,499 for 3 months · about ₹833 a month · + ₹449.82 GST". Checkout then shows "₹2,948.82". Home says "From ₹500 a month", which is ₹590 once GST is added.
- **Why:** CCPA's Dark Patterns Guidelines (2023) name *drip pricing* (research §5.2), and Indian consumer pricing is normally shown inclusive of tax. Paise amounts like ₹449.82 look like a spreadsheet, and "about ₹833" understates the real ₹983.
- **Fix:** lead with the all-inclusive price rounded to whole rupees (₹2,949 for 3 months, about ₹983 a month, incl. 18% GST). Put the GST split in a small line and on the invoice. Show renewal behaviour ("does not renew automatically" or the 24-hour pre-debit notice per RBI) and the refund policy next to the button.

### P1-4: Assisted is sold without any commitment
- **/plans and Home.** Assisted at ₹19,999 lists "Everything in Gold, and", then repeats all five Gold items, then "A dedicated relationship manager". Nothing is promised: no introductions per month, no callback time, no refund if they are missed. "Choose Assisted" is plain text with no button.
- **Why:** research §5.3 decided that Assisted must be sold only with published commitments and a pro-rated refund. Shaadi VIP, BharatMatrimony Assisted and Jeevansathi Exclusive draw the "paid, then nothing" anger (52 of 129 reviews).
- **Fix:** list only what Assisted adds, for example "A named relationship manager · N hand-picked introductions a month · first call within 24 hours · pro-rated refund if we miss it". Give it a real secondary button.

### P1-5: the plans section sits on Home, against the research decision
- **Home, both seats, bottom.** Three full plan cards (Free boxed in red as CURRENT, Gold, Assisted), repeated from /plans.
- **Why:** research §5.3 was "Never on Home as a banner or card… nagging is a named dark pattern". Home is the family's desk. On a phone the cards take about 450px, more than Your search.
- **Fix:** replace them with one quiet line in the Your search block: "Free · 2 of 3 requests left today · Plans". Show the upsell only when the limit is hit.

### P1-6: light mode is cream, not turmeric

### P1-7: green is used for states, outside the palette
- **Profile ("Request sent" bar, all "✓" chips), /plans (Free card outlined green, green ticks).** On Home the same "current plan" state is outlined **red**. The same state has two colours, and neither is calm.

### P1-8: the horoscope rings are all red, and a full ring looks like a warning
- **Profile → Horoscope tab (Guna view).** Eight red progress rings. Nadi 8/8 is a full red circle, which reads as danger although it is the best score.

### P1-9: the introduction card is headed by a number, not a person
- **Home family seat, Discover, profile.** When a member hides their name, the heading is just "30 yrs". The pending request tile shows only a face and "30 yrs". The row title reads "More like this 30-year-old Senior Software Engineer".
- **Fix:** use a human handle that keeps the name private: occupation plus city as the title ("Software engineer, Hyderabad"), with age beside it. Or use a stable profile ID as Shaadi and BharatMatrimony do (e.g. "MG-4A33"). Row title: "More like the engineer you sent a request to".

### P1-10 FUNCTIONAL: two stores for "what you're looking for" disagree

### P1-11: the matchmaker page is a long classic form again
- **/me/matchmaker, 390.** Own-words box, then "What we understood", then 6 themes, then 6 "In your partner" scales, then 6 "About you" scales: 18 "Set" rows on a 3,200px page. The Save button floats mid-page after "What we understood", so it is unclear whether it saves the themes and scales below it. "ADD A POINT" shows an empty-looking control on screen.

Move "About you" scales to the profile (they describe the member, not the wish). Use one sticky Save at the bottom that covers everything. Make "Add a point" a visible chip row.

### P1-12: the compatibility panel has an empty avatar and duplicates
- **Profile, candidate seat.** The left "you" avatar is a blank dark disc: Asha has no photo, and her initials "AR" are used elsewhere but not here. "Both hold a master's level of education" (Why you might work) repeats "Also studied to master's level" (Also in common).
