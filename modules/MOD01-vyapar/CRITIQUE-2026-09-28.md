# Vyapar UX critique — 2026-09-28 build (FE1 + FE2)

**Reviewer:** Vyapar standing UX critique agent (Upwork / WorkIndia / Apna / Job Hai lens).
**Scope:** the two parallel frontend workstreams landing in `vyapar-web/`:
- **FE1:** posting composer with AI drafting and voice, structured proposal and one-tap apply, applicants board, taxonomy picker.
- **FE2:** Call/WhatsApp contact actions and preferences, badges, seeker protection, service packages, working Discover filters and saved searches, contracts and milestones, desktop Hire/Find-work menu, `/outcomes` pages.

**Rules applied to every screen:** a structured composer for each action, never a generic chat box ·
one tap for both sides · a real desktop layout, not a phone column · en/hi/te with no hardcoded
English · near-monochrome with one accent (`DESIGN-ENHANCEMENT.md`) · no extra intermediate
screens · would a delivery worker in Hyderabad and a CA in Mumbai both succeed?

**Evidence sources:** `references/2026-screen-research.md` (cited as "R§n"),
`references/imp-backend-{a,b,c,d}.md` (API contracts), the owner's screenshots in
`references/workindia/` and `references/upwork/`, and research labelled per finding.

**Ranking:** Blocker = a user cannot finish the task, or an owner rule is broken ·
Major = the task finishes but the product is wrong or confusing · Minor = polish.

## 2030 lens: how people will expect it to work

Habits formed by UPI, Reels/Instagram, WhatsApp, Swiggy/Zepto, ChatGPT/voice assistants and
ixigo/Airbnb browsing:
- The first screen *is* content.
- One thumb, with actions at the bottom.
- Choices are pictures or chips, not forms.
- Speaking works everywhere.
- Status is visible like an order tracker.
- Money is shown as ₹ in the words people use.
- Nothing asks for what the app already knows.

Anything that reads as a 2015 web form, a desktop sidebar of checkboxes, or a wall of text is a
finding.

## Live findings (pass 4, 2030 lens), owners named

### W1 — Major (fails 9 user types) — FE2 / DESIGN-LEAD — on a phone, Discover's first screen has no opportunity in it
- **Where:** `screenshots/2026-09-29/fe2-discover-rows-mobile-dark-01.png` (390 × 844). From
  the top:
  - a "Namaste, Suresh" heading;
  - the Opportunities | Businesses switch;
  - the search field;
  - the location chip;
  - a Filters + Sort row;
  - a chip row.

  The first card's title is below the fold (about 1,000px down in that capture, whose device
  scale is 2). `app/page.tsx` and `DiscoverView.tsx` stack all six controls above the rows.
- **2030 evidence:** ixigo's own page (the owner's reference, seen live; see "Owner direction 2")
  puts one compact search bar and then the first row of cards on the first screen. Instagram,
  Swiggy and Zepto open on content. A rider checks between drops and gives a screen about a
  second.
- **Fix:**
  - Merge the location chip *into* the search field, as its trailing chip ("Dilsukhnagar ·
    12 km").
  - Replace the Filters/Sort row with one filter icon at the end of the quick-chip row.
  - Move the greeting onto the language/bell line, or drop it.
  - **Target:** the first card's title and pay are visible within 844px at 390 wide, and at
    360 × 740 (common ₹10k phones).

### W2 — Major (fails U3, U6) — FE1 (copy) + backend B — "your number is never shown outside [your hours]" is not true
- **Where:** `vyapar-web/locales/en/hiring.json` → `precall.explain`: "Your call hours and daily
  call limit still apply, and your number is never shown outside them." The pre-accept Call is a
  `tel:+91…` href built from the real number (`vyapar-service/app/services/contact.py:459,656`).
  After one call, the caller has it in their call log for good, and can call or WhatsApp at any
  hour outside Vyapar.
- **Who fails:**
  - Meena (home tailor; VISION-2040 §1 #3 says she "never shares her address before an order is
    confirmed");
  - Sarita (home tutor);
  - any woman posting a local-service need from her own phone.

  VISION-2040 §0: 42% of WhatsApp users have received job-scam messages. A number given to
  strangers is how those scams reach people.
- **Fix:**
  - Say it plainly on the switch: "Anyone who calls will see your phone number."
  - Offer "WhatsApp only" as a middle setting.
  - Keep the default off, as it is now.
  - Log a masked/proxy number (Exotel/Knowlarity class; the vendor blocker already named in R§4)
    as the production path.
  - Tie this to VISION-2040 item 9 (women-safe mode).

### W3 — Major (fails U1, U2, U4, U5) — FE2 / DESIGN-LEAD — the Discover card is a text block with no visual anchor, and pay mixes "LPA" with "a month"
- **Where:** `vyapar-web/app/components/discover/DiscoverCard.tsx:51-79`. Each card has a kind
  chip, title, pay, place, "posted …", badges and "Why this?": six or seven lines of text, no
  icon, and "Why this?" on every card. In
  `screenshots/2026-09-29/fe2-discover-filters-desktop-light-01.png`, one job reads "₹8–10 LPA"
  next to another reading "₹18,000 to ₹24,000 a month".
- **2030 evidence:**
  - WorkIndia's card (owner screenshot `references/workindia/02-similar-jobs.jpg`) scans on three
    things: role, salary range, distance.
  - The low-literacy research cited in the palette doc (arXiv 2601.17025) says abstract icons
    fail alone and words matter. So the answer is *a recognisable trade pictogram plus the word*.
  - "LPA" is recruiter jargon a rider cannot read.
- **Fix:**
  - Lead each card with the trade pictogram from the taxonomy category. The `icon` field already
    exists in the tree (`imp-backend-a.md:338`).
  - Make the pay line the largest text, always "a month" for jobs (LPA ÷ 12 → "₹67,000–₹83,000 a
    month").
  - Show distance as a chip ("4.4 km").
  - Move "Why this?" to the detail page.
  - Keep the card to four lines at most.

### W4 — Major (fails U6, U7, U8) — FE2 / DESIGN-LEAD — the desktop Discover home is a checkbox sidebar beside browsing rows
- **Where:** `screenshots/2026-09-29/fe2-discover-filters-desktop-light-01.png`. The unfiltered
  home shows a 248px "Filters" rail (checkboxes, Min/Max rupee boxes, radio buttons) beside the
  themed rows.
- **2030 evidence:**
  - ixigo shows no filter rail on its browsing home, only on results after a search.
  - Airbnb puts filters behind one "Filters" button over a visual grid.
  - Baymard's rail guidance (R§6) applies to *results* pages.

  On a browsing home, a column of checkboxes is the "2015 web form" pattern.
- **Fix:**
  - Hide the rail while rows are shown, and let the rows use the full width (about 5 cards per
    row at 1440).
  - Show the rail only in the filtered results mode, which `app/page.tsx` already switches to.

## Design language 2026-09-29 (DESIGN-LEAD): judged against U1–U12 and the 2030 lens

`references/design-language-2026-09-29.md` read in full. At the time of this pass it is a spec
only: `app/styles/language-2026.css`, `nav/AppTopBar.tsx`, `discover/trades.tsx` and the
`screenshots/2026-09-29/design-*` captures do not exist yet. These are verdicts on the spec, to
re-check against the screens.

**What it gets right, by user type:**
- **Trade tiles with a word under every icon** (§7–8), reused as the icon on every card. Covers
  U1, U4 and U5, and matches the low-literacy evidence (icon + word, never alone).
- **The pay line as the hero** of each card (§10). Covers U1 and U6.
- **No permanent filter sidebar**: a bottom sheet on phones and a right drawer on desktop (§2.3,
  §11). This resolves **W4** once built.
- **Text size A+ / A++, high contrast, rem everywhere, Indic line height 1.6, no text under 13px,
  44/48px targets** (§3, §5, §9). Covers U4, U5 and U12.
- **Reduced motion honoured; motion only explains a change** (§12). No gradients, glass or glow;
  shadows are never colour.
- The six tile tints are *meaning* (family of work), each contrast-checked. That is acceptable
  under the one-brand rule, because none is used for actions.

### D1 — Major (fails 9 user types, makes W1 worse) — DESIGN-LEAD — the phone Discover spec pushes the first opportunity further down
- **Where:** §11 "Phone", items 1–5. The stack is:
  1. the 56px top bar;
  2. a hero (a 2rem greeting, a quiet line, the Opportunities/Businesses icon tabs, the search
     pill);
  3. a trade rail of 64px tiles with 2-line labels;
  4. sticky quick chips;
  5. and only then the rows.

  At 390 × 844, and certainly at 360 × 740 (a ₹10k phone), the first card's title and pay land
  below the fold. That is further down than today's screen (W1).
- **2030 evidence:** ixigo (seen live) opens with the search bar and then cards. Instagram,
  Swiggy and Zepto open on content. Apna's own study (cited in §1) found people "didn't know how
  many steps were left", and on a home screen the equivalent is "where is the work?".
- **Fix:** add an **above-the-fold budget** to §11: at 360 × 740, the first row's title and one
  full card (title + pay) must be visible without scrolling. To meet it:
  - put the greeting on one line inside the top bar, or remove it;
  - move the Opportunities/Businesses tabs into the top bar;
  - use the trade rail *or* the quick chips on the first screen, not both (chips can sit
    directly under the rail and scroll away with it).

### D2 — Major — DESIGN-LEAD — the new input spec still fails WCAG 1.4.11 (P1 not fixed)
- **Where:** §13: inputs get a "white surface; default border". `--border-default` is `#cfccc3`
  (`globals.css:61`), which is **1.61:1** on white (my computation). WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.11 requires
  3:1 for the edge that identifies a control. The high-contrast mode (§9) fixes it only for
  people who turn that mode on. The default must pass on its own.
- **Fix:** give inputs, selects, text areas, secondary buttons and unselected chips
  `--border-strong` (`#807c72`, 4.16:1 on white), or a new `--border-control` of at least 3:1.
  Add those pairs to `check-contrast.mjs`.

### D3 — Minor — DESIGN-LEAD + FE2 — the spec's own card example uses "₹9–14 LPA"
- **Where:** §10 card anatomy. "LPA" is recruiter jargon that U1, U4 and U5 cannot read (W3).
- **Fix:** jobs always show pay "a month" (annual ÷ 12); projects show "Fixed price" or "an hour"
  (the backend's own `compensation_label` forms, `imp-backend-a.md:127-136`). Update the
  example.

### D4 — Minor — DESIGN-LEAD — two brand-filled buttons on the Discover first screen break the spec's own rule 2
- **Where:** §11 puts a "round brand search button" in the hero search pill *and* a "filled brand
  circle" Post in the hub, on the same screen. Rule 2 (§2) says only one button per screen is
  filled with the brand colour.
- **Fix:** on Discover, the search button is ink (`--selected-solid-bg`) and Post keeps the brand
  fill. Or keep search as the brand fill and outline Post there. Pick one.

### P1 — Major — design-system agent (affects FE1/FE2 forms) — text fields, dropdowns and secondary buttons have almost invisible edges (1.3:1)
- **Where:** `vyapar-web/app/globals.css:823-834`: `.vy-input`, `.vy-select`, `.vy-textarea` use
  `border: 1px solid var(--border-subtle)`, which is `#e3e1da` in light mode (`:60`), with a
  `--surface` #f6f5f1 fill. `.vy-btn-secondary` (`:792-796`) uses the same border.
  `.vy-chip` (unselected) uses `--border-hairline` (same value).
- **Measured:**

  | Edge | Contrast | Minimum |
  |---|---|---|
  | Border on a white card | **1.31:1** | 3:1 |
  | Input fill against a white card | **1.09:1** | — |
  | `--border-default` (if swapped in) | 1.61:1 | 3:1 |
  | `--border-strong` #807c72 | **4.16:1** | 3:1, passes |

  The palette doc calls hairline and default borders "decorative separators"
  (`palette-2026-09-29.md` §3), so the script never checks them. Yet they are the *only*
  visual boundary of every form control. Visible in `screenshots/2026-09-29/palette-after-composer-390-light.png`:
  the "Describe what you need" box is a faint outline even on the canvas, and inside white cards
  (the proposal composer, preferences, contract forms) the fields almost vanish.
- **Evidence:** WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast requires 3:1 for "visual information required
  to identify user interface components"
  ([w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/non-text-contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html),
  cited by the palette doc itself). For a first-time smartphone user, a field that doesn't look
  like a box doesn't look tappable.
- **Fix:**
  - Give `.vy-input`, `.vy-select`, `.vy-textarea`, `.vy-btn-secondary` and unselected `.vy-chip`
    a border of `--border-strong` (or a new `--border-control` of at least 3:1 on both
    `--surface` and `--surface-raised`).
  - Give inputs a `--surface-raised` (white) fill on the canvas.
  - Add those pairs to `check-contrast.mjs`, so "decorative" can't silently cover a control edge
    again.
- **Status:** open (pass 3).

### P2 — Minor — design-system agent — "disabled" is only 45% opacity, with no reason given
- **Where:** `globals.css:804-807` (`.vy-btn:disabled { opacity: 0.45 }`). In the composer
  screenshot, a disabled "Draft it" is a pale lilac pill beside a white "Read from a photo". A
  less tech-literate user reads it as a different style, not as "not yet".
- **Fix:** keep the opacity, and give each disabled primary action its reason in a line under it
  ("Type or speak what you need first"). The composer's `describeHint` slot could carry it.
- **Status:** open (pass 3).
