# Vyapar palette, 2026-09-29

**Why:** the owner said on 2026-09-29: "colors need to be changed in vyapar, i really hate the colors you used, and theme based designs, else all other is good." This replaces the saffron-on-near-black, dark-first palette. Layout, type, spacing and motion are unchanged.

**Where it lives:** `vyapar-web/app/globals.css` (tokens), `vyapar-web/scripts/check-contrast.mjs` (the contrast proof) and `DESIGN-ENHANCEMENT.md` → "Palette 2026-09-29" (the rules).

## 1. Research: what trusted products do

| Product | What we saw | Principle we took |
|---|---|---|
| **ixigo** (hotels page; the owner's reference) | Measured live in our browser: canvas `#F4F5F5`, white cards, text `#17181C`, secondary `#5E616E`. Orange `#FC790D` appears **only** on the one Search button and "View all" links. Blue `#0770E4` is used for selected tabs and links, with a light-blue tint `#D9EBFF` for selection and green `#238C46` for success. Brand colours per [Brandfetch](https://brandfetch.com/ixigo.com). | Use a light canvas with white cards and dark ink. The brand colour appears once per screen, on the main action. Card titles are ink, never brand. |
| **Upwork** (the Vyapar model) | Green and black on white. ([Mobbin brand colours](https://mobbin.com/colors/brand/upwork-global-inc)) | Use one confident brand colour on a white, work-focused canvas, with green reserved for verified and trust. |
| **Zerodha Kite** | White is the default background, and a dark theme is an optional setting ([Z-Connect](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/kite/dark-theme-simplified-order-window-on-kite-web), [learnstockmarket](https://www.learnstockmarket.in/brokers/zerodha/change-background-colour/)). | Default to light and offer dark as a setting. Serious money apps in India are light by default. |
| **PhonePe** | A single purple on white. They made it warmer and richer because the purple "tended to fade to a blue-ish shade over time. This is a peculiar Indian problem" ([The Hard Copy](https://thehardcopy.co/phonepes-brand-identity-case-study/)). | One saturated brand hue on white. Choose a brand colour that stays rich on cheap, bright phone screens, so no pale or greyed blues. |
| **Urban Company** | Black and white, chosen "because they communicate simplicity" ([Urban Company blog](https://www.urbancompany.com/blog/when-looks-do-matter-the-story-behind-our-new-logo)). | The neutral ink/white system carries most of the UI. Selected states can be ink, not brand. |
| **Swiggy** | Orange brand, with tokens that allow a theme change "with one line of code" ([Obvious case study](https://www.obvious.in/swiggy)). General guidance: "avoid overusing brand colours or using them on large surfaces" ([Orange design system](https://unified-design-system.orange.com/472794e18/p/217ac6-colour)). | Tokens only. Never put the brand colour on large surfaces such as a full-width slab. |
| **CRED** | NeoPOP: neon colours on a deep black canvas ([neopop-web](https://github.com/CRED-CLUB/neopop-web)). | This is the look the owner rejected: a dark, themed, "cool" canvas. It suits a premium credit-card audience, not a CA and a delivery rider together. |
| **Linear** | Moved to near-monochrome with "even fewer bold colors" and generates themes from three variables in LCH ([Linear](https://linear.app/now/how-we-redesigned-the-linear-ui), [LogRocket](https://blog.logrocket.com/ux-design/linear-design/)). | Neutrals do the work and colour is functional. Themes come from a few tokens. |
| **Stripe** | Designed its colour system in a perceptually uniform space so that every hue passes contrast and stays vivid ([Stripe blog](https://stripe.com/blog/accessible-color-systems)). | Pick each semantic colour to pass AA against every surface, then verify with a script rather than by eye. |

**Accessibility and culture**

- WCAG 2.2 AA requires 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text and 3:1 for UI components and states, including focus rings and control edges ([W3C 1.4.11](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html), [Deque](https://dequeuniversity.com/resources/wcag2.1/1.4.11-non-text-contrast)).
- For less tech-literate users, never let colour carry meaning alone. Pair it with a word or icon ([AccessGuide](https://www.accessguide.io/guide/colorblind)). Research on low-literacy users shows abstract icons are often opaque, so the words matter ([arXiv 2601.17025](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.17025)).
- In Indian culture, blue carries calm, depth and divinity (Krishna, Shiva), and green carries growth, auspiciousness and the flag's India Green ([color-meanings](https://www.color-meanings.com/color-meanings-indian-culture/), [Mobbin India Green](https://mobbin.com/colors/meaning/india-green)). There is also a warning that a Western "cold" blue reads as distant to Indian users ([India in Design](https://indiaindesign.medium.com/why-do-indian-color-palettes-feel-different-004549531797)). That is why the brand colour is a warm-leaning indigo, *neel*, the colour of India's historic indigo dye, on a warm off-white rather than a cold grey. Red is kept for danger only.

## 2. The decision

- **Canvas:** warm "chalk" `#f6f5f1` with white cards. It is light and clean, not plain grey, and it separates cards without heavy shadows.
- **Primary: Neel indigo** `#2b4acb` (dark mode `#8b9dff`). It reads as trustworthy and professional (the blue family used by banks and payment apps), stays rich on bright phone screens, and is clearly different from the rejected saffron. It is used only for the primary action, links, focus and the current-page marker.
- **Secondary: peacock teal** `#0e7470` (text `#0e6b67`), for small highlights only (icon tiles, profile monogram). It is warm and Indian (peacock), and distinct from success green.
- **Semantic:** success green, amber warning, red danger, blue info and verified green. Each has a solid value, a `-soft` tint and a `-text` value.
- **Selected state = neutral ink** (`--selected-*`). The old palette put the accent on every selected chip. Now a selected chip gets an ink outline on a light fill, the way Airbnb, Google and Urban Company show choices.
- **Card titles = `--text-primary`.**
- **Dark mode** follows the system setting and is a blue-slate night (`#121419`), not near-black. `data-theme` can force either theme.
- **No gradients, glass or glow.** The action bar's blur and the top bar's gradient were replaced with solid surfaces.

## 3. Tokens and contrast

Every value below is read from `globals.css` by `node scripts/check-contrast.mjs`, which checks 216 pairs:

- every text colour on all six surfaces
- the brand, focus ring, strong border and selected border as UI colours on all six surfaces
- text on its own tint
- text on the brand fill
- selected text on the selected fill

## 4. What changed in components (colour declarations only)

- **globals.css:**
  - Chip selected state is now neutral.
  - Primary hover uses `--brand-hover`.
  - Focus outlines use `--focus-ring`.
  - `.vy-error` uses `--danger-text`.
  - The deadline-soon text uses `--warning-text`.
  - The monogram and choice icon tiles use `--secondary-*`.
  - `.vy-card-selected` uses `--selected-border`.
  - Skeleton bars use `--surface-sunken` so they show on white cards.
  - The action bar is solid (no blur) and the top scrim is solid (no gradient).
  - Added a zero-specificity card-title rule and `.vy-search-action`.
- **IntentBar.tsx:** added the `vy-search-action` class to the Search button, so it is not a full-width slab from 600px up.
- **Selected chips and options changed from accent to `--selected-*`:** ReportSheet, Reputation, ReviewComposer, Verification (plus its scrim, now `--scrim`), LanguageSwitcher, DevMemberSwitcher, admin/moderation, first-run.
- **Segmented toggles changed from accent fill to `--selected-solid-*`:** boost, workspace, ReviewComposer, EnquiriesView, first-run language, CommandHub tabs.
- **Picked plan/product cards:** now use `--selected-border`.
- **hiring.css (FE1):**
  - Focus rings use `--focus-ring`.
  - Picker category, picker spec, applicant-selected and stage tabs use `--selected-*`.
  - The sheet backdrop uses `--scrim`.
- **fe2.css (FE2):**
  - Package tabs, contract row, mega-menu category and bell toggle use `--selected-*`.
  - The sheet scrim uses `--scrim`.
  - The held dot uses `--text-tertiary`.
  - Danger text uses `--danger-text`.
- **Sweep result:** no hex, rgb, hsl or named colours remain in any `.tsx` or `.css` outside the token blocks. The one exception is `app/page.module.css`, which is the unused create-next-app template: nothing imports it.
