## 1. Intent
"Quiet Luxury + Human Network": warm ivory canvas, deep charcoal ink, one deep-teal accent, editorial serif headlines, fine hairlines, shallow depth. Light, dark and reduced-motion supported. Note: the 6 Oct critique (C18) asked the owner to confirm Counsel's look because the owner rejected dark themed looks for Vyapar; today the light theme is the default and dark follows the OS or the You-page choice (the critique saw it near-black; unverified which default the owner reviewed).

## 3. Typography
Display (h1-h3, serif numbers): Fraunces with Georgia fallback; body: Anek Latin, Anek Devanagari (Hindi, Marathi), Anek Telugu, Anek Kannada, system-ui. Body 16px / 1.5. h1 clamp(2rem, 4.2vw, 3.1rem), h2 clamp(1.45rem, 2.6vw, 1.9rem), h3 1.2rem, letter spacing -0.01em. Small, tiny and eyebrow styles are utility classes.

## 5. Layout and responsive
Mobile first. `.wrap` 16px gutters (32px from 900px). Breakpoints: 640px (2-column grids), 900px (top nav shown, bottom bar hidden, 3/4-column grids, larger padding), 960px (two-column pages with a 360px right rail). Bottom bar fixed with four tabs; page bottom padding reserves it. Room hides both bars.

## 6. Motion
Button press translateY(1px), 0.12 s transitions, `fade-in` 0.35 s on revealed cards, shimmer skeleton, speaking indicator and net banner in the room; all animation and transitions disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## 7. Theming and accessibility
Theme choice persisted in `localStorage` (`counsel.theme`: auto/light/dark). Warm palette: contrast ratios were not measured (unverified; verify with a contrast checker, especially muted #7a7469 on #f5f0e8 and the amber warn tag). Focus-visible outline 2px accent. Language-specific fonts avoid fallback boxes for Indian scripts.

## 8. Open design points
Dark default question (C18); duplicated lists on Home ("People you have consulted" and "Trusted people") flagged C12; filters take much of the first mobile screen on Discover (C12); disclaimers appear only at payment (C16).
