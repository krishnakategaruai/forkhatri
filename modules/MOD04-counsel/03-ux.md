## 2. Per-screen specification
**SCR-01 Home** (seen live). Eyebrow "COUNSEL", H1 "What do you need help with?", lede about paying for an hour with someone who has done it, search form "Describe it in your own words..." + "Find someone", chips "Review my startup idea", "GST for freelance income from abroad", "Plan my first Himalayan trek"; optional upcoming card; "Browse by area" tiles (eight shown, "N people"); "People you have consulted"; "Trusted people" cards; "Know something people would pay to learn? ... ForKhatri keeps 2%" with "Become a consultant". States: skeleton while loading. Out: Discover with `need` or `area`, profile, Provide.
**SCR-02 Discover.** Search bar with filter button (count badge) and Search; "understood" strip (tags + Clear) when arrived from a need; toolbar with city select (Anywhere / Use my location / 45 cities) and sort select; quick chips (Available now, Free in next 24 hours, 4 star and above, Within 150 km of city); area chips; collapsible filter card (price min-max, language, rating, verified only, Apply, Clear); count and results grid (2 columns wide); notes about nearness and Bayesian rating. States: skeleton, empty with reset action. URL carries all filters.
**SCR-03 Profile.** Left: avatar, area eyebrow, name, headline, rating stars, city, languages, Save toggle, Report (signed in). Sections About, Why trust, Services (price, minutes, formats, outcomes, Book), Reviews. Right rail: at-a-glance, reply time, dimension averages, milestone badges with dates, weekly availability with time zone, Book CTA. Report dialog (misleading, unqualified, abuse, spam, other). Non-approved shows a status tag.
**SCR-04 Book.** Step 1 time: day tabs (30 days) and slot grid in viewer time zone, "Propose a time" card when allowed (date, time, note), brief textarea, Continue (or sign in). Step 2 confirm: summary rows, price, fee note "Includes ForKhatri platform fee (2%)", "You pay", cancellation tiers, disclaimer in warning colour, platform role statement, agreement checkbox, Back, "Pay and confirm". Footer note says test payment succeeds instantly and no money moves. Right: consultant card, outcomes, price, available-now tag, own package use, follow-up offer, Ask a question. Errors inline; slot conflicts reset to step 1.
**SCR-05 Bookings.** Header, summary (needs action, today, this week), Coming up, past list, pending time requests (to you and yours), purchases (follow-ups, questions).
**SCR-06 Booking detail.** Header with date badge, status tag, relative time, service, counterpart, time range with zone, duration, voice/video; banners for held payment (Pay now), room ready (Join), review ask; brief; conversation with system messages, files as links; follow-up offers; recap; review; dispute; timeline; right rail money (price, fee, you receive for consultant, payout, refunded), cancellation tiers with current tier bold, actions (Add to calendar, Mark complete, No-show after 10 min, Cancel, Dispute, Book again). Dialogs: cancel (shows refund), review (5 star inputs), dispute.
**SCR-07 Room.** Full-screen call surface with pre-join check (camera/mic choice, voice-only option), remote and draggable self-view, controls (mic, camera, share, record, PiP, full screen, devices, leave, recording consent), network banner, side panel tabs for chat, shared note, private note, actions, files; post-call "How was the call quality?" with issue chips. Closed state before open window and after +2 h.
**SCR-08 Notifications.** List of up to 50 sentences, unread bold, link to booking, marks read on open; empty state.
**SCR-09 Saved.** Cards or empty with Discover action.
**SCR-10 You.** Avatar and name, language chips (5), appearance chips (Auto/Light/Dark), links (consultant dashboard or become consultant, Saved, ForKhatri account, back to hub), Privacy card (Download my data, Delete my Counsel data -> dialog listing blockers or DELETE confirmation), Sign out (normal, not everywhere).
**SCR-11 Overview.** Not-a-consultant start card; status card with Submit; checklist (profile, review, availability, service); trust tags; Available now toggle (30/60/120 min); questions to answer; follow-up sales; time requests; upcoming preview; How you are found (stats, strength meter with fix links, standing text); badges and next milestones; performance stats; earnings tiles.
**SCR-12 Bookings (consultant).** Board with role filter provider.
**SCR-13 Profile.** Photo upload, name note, headline, intro, city picker or other town, country, languages, links, expertise rows (area, years, primary), propose new area, evidence cards by kind with proof upload and status, Save, Submit for review.
**SCR-14 Services.** List, add/edit form (title, description, area, duration, price in rupees, formats, advanced: notice, advance, buffer, recording, custom times), extras dialog (follow-up offers, written-question price/days, instant). Fee note: "ForKhatri keeps 2%".
**SCR-15 Availability.** Weekly list with Add window / Add fixed time per day, exceptions (date, day off or extra hours, note), time zone shown.
**SCR-16 Earnings.** Ready-for-payout card with Request payout, payout method editor (UPI or bank, masked after save), transactions list (net, gross minus fee), payouts list.
**SCR-17..24 Admin.** Overview queue tiles and economics; providers by status; provider review (public view link, evidence with View proof, per-evidence verify/reject, per-dimension verification, decision with note, services, badge history, audit); expertise requests (approve with slug, names, regulated) and taxonomy; reports (Actioned/Dismiss); disputes (refund %, resolution); transactions; settings (fee %, cancellation tiers and rules, payout hold hours, retention days).
**SCR-25 Gate.** Card "Sign in to ForKhatri" with button to hub (`return_to`), or "Can't reach ForKhatri sign-in" with Retry.

## 3. Key flows
Seeker: Home -> need or area -> Discover -> Profile -> Book step 1 -> step 2 -> Booking detail (new) -> reminders -> Room -> Completed -> recap, review, follow-up offer. Consultant: You -> Consult -> Profile (+evidence) -> Submit -> wait -> approved -> Services + Availability -> bookings -> Room -> complete -> Earnings -> payout method -> Request payout. Custom time: Book -> Propose -> consultant accepts on Overview/Bookings -> seeker pays within 24 h. Admin: Providers queue -> review -> verify dimensions -> approve.

## 4. States (code-derived)
Loading skeletons on every list; empty states with a next action; error toasts using `errors.<code>`; held-payment countdown; room closed; reconnecting and weak-connection banners; AI "understood" strip; deletion blocked list. Dev note on Book states the payment is a test.

## 5. Accessibility and responsive
Skip: none observed. Landmarks: banner, main, navigation "Main". Filter button has `aria-expanded`; chips use `aria-pressed`; nav `aria-current="page"`; slot day tabs use `role=tab`/`aria-selected`; progress bar has `role=progressbar`; sr-only labels on selects; `prefers-reduced-motion` disables animation; focus ring 2 px accent. Mobile first: bottom bar below 900 px, two-column layouts at wide widths. Known: Telugu/Hindi font stacks via Anek; no automated accessibility audit run (unverified).

## 6. Account deletion screens (added 2026-11-22, as built; NOT browser-checked)
- **SCR-10 You page (`counsel-web/app/me/page.tsx`)**, card "Your data": buttons "Download my data" and a red "Delete my data in Counsel" (`privacy.delete`).
- **Delete dialog** (`Dialog`, title "Delete my data in Counsel?", `privacy.delete_title`): plain-words list of what is deleted; a "Kept without your name" paragraph (`privacy.keeps`: consultations other people took part in, payment and tax records where money moved, star ratings you gave (no text), and a safety record of reports); a "This will also happen right now:" list filled from `GET /me/deletion-check`; field "Type DELETE to confirm"; red button "Delete permanently" enabled only when the typed text equals DELETE; "Keep it" cancels. On success a toast "Your Counsel data was deleted" and the browser goes to the ForKhatri entrance.
- Other people see "Deleted member" in place of the name on consultations, reviews and chat. Hub-side screens (account sheet, progress, receipt) are in `docs/ParentApp/ACCOUNT-DELETION.md`.
- Flow: You page -> dialog -> type DELETE -> erase -> entrance.
