# Counsel backlog

Last updated: 1 Oct 2026.

This file lists what is still open after the Counsel build of 30 Sep 2026, which followed the Counsel product definition and a review of eight competitors. Everything else in that build is done and was checked on screen. Each item says what already exists on our side and what would unblock it.

## Built from the competitor review (30 Sep 2026)

These came from comparing Counsel with Topmate, Practo, LawRato, Clarity.fm, Intro.co, MentorCruise, GrowthMentor and JustAnswer. All of them are done and checked on screen.

| What | Where it came from | How it works in Counsel |
|---|---|---|
| **Follow-ups** (reshaped from session packages, 30 Sep 2026) | MentorCruise, Topmate bundles; reshaped because Counsel is advice, not tuition | After a completed consultation, a consultant can offer 1–3 short check-ins on the same problem, used within 30, 60 or 90 days, for example 2 follow-ups for ₹2,000 instead of ₹2,400. They are offered only to people who have already had a consultation with that consultant, on the booking page ("Continue with …") and on the service page. They must cost less than booking the same sessions one by one. Paid once; each check-in is then booked with nothing to pay. Cancelling a check-in 24 or more hours ahead gives it back. Unused follow-ups are refunded automatically when they end or are cancelled. The consultant is paid per completed check-in. |
| **Paid written questions** | Topmate Priority DM, JustAnswer | The consultant sets a question price and an answer time of 1–7 days. If the question is not answered in time, the full price is refunded automatically. The consultant is paid once they answer. Each person can have at most two open questions with a consultant. |
| **Instant consults ("Available now")** | Practo, Astrotalk | The consultant goes online for 30, 60 or 120 minutes. On services where they allow it, the next 5-minute start becomes bookable. Discover has an "Available now" filter, and cards show "Can start now". |
| **Call polish** | Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams | A self-view that can be dragged to any corner, picture-in-picture (a button, and automatically when switching tabs in Chrome where the browser allows it), camera and microphone pickers that swap the device mid-call without redialling, and a quick "How was the call quality?" after leaving, with issue chips (sound, video, screen share, dropping, echo). Answers are stored per booking (`call_feedback`) for improving the room. |
| **Recap after every call** | GrowthMentor (recaps) | When a consultation is completed, both people get one recap on the booking page with the shared notes, action items and files. An AI summary is added on top only when Claude is switched on (T6). |
| **Add to calendar** | Topmate calendar sync (the simpler form) | Every live booking has a calendar invite file, with a join link and a reminder 15 minutes before. |
| **Propose a time** | Intro.co | Built earlier: the person proposes a time, the consultant accepts or declines, and nothing is charged unless it is accepted. |

## Blocked outside the team

| # | Item | What's built on our side | What unblocks it |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **Real payments, refunds and payouts** (the only blocker to going live) | Now built on ForKhatri's shared integrations library (`shared/forkhatri-integrations`, 1 Oct 2026): `PAYMENT_PROVIDER=sandbox` or `payment_services` (future MOD06) goes through it, with signed payment events at `POST /payments/events`; `mock` stays the default. `PaymentProvider` interface (`counsel-service/app/components/money.py`) with create, verify, refund and payout calls. A mock adapter that succeeds locally and is refused unless the API runs on this machine. A ledger that covers bookings, packages and questions: gross, platform fee (2%, a setting), refunds, consultant payable and payouts. Bookings snapshot the price and the cancellation rules at payment. | Choose a gateway (Razorpay, Cashfree or Stripe) and get merchant and payout (route or marketplace) accounts. Then write one adapter, add a webhook route that calls `confirm_payment` for bookings and `purchases.pay` for packages and questions, and set `PAYMENT_PROVIDER`. The adapter should also report the gateway's processing fee, which the ledger already has a column for. |
| B2 | **WhatsApp, SMS, email and push delivery** | Queued deliveries are now sent every minute by the shared library's dispatcher with the senders named in `SMS_PROVIDER`, `WHATSAPP_PROVIDER`, `EMAIL_PROVIDER` and `PUSH_PROVIDER` (console or disabled until a vendor is chosen). Rows are marked skipped until the platform offers a contact lookup (shared BACKLOG B3). Every notification is a domain event: bookings, reminders, time requests, packages, questions, recaps, badges, payouts, disputes, and consultant approval. They go to the in-app inbox now. The `email`, `sms`, `whatsapp` and `push` channels queue a row in the `delivery` table when listed in `NOTIFICATION_CHANNELS`. | Provider accounts, for example a WhatsApp Business API provider, an SMS gateway with DLT registration, and an email sender. Then add a small dispatcher that sends queued `delivery` rows with retries. Or use the platform's shared notification service once ForKhatri has one. WhatsApp reminders are what Topmate advertises most. |
| B3 | **Professional register checks** (doctor, lawyer, CA, investment adviser) | Evidence is recorded separately as experience, qualification, certification, registration, business and portfolio. Private proof documents can be attached. Reviewers verify each dimension separately, and every decision and every document view is audited. Compliance rules per area and country stop a service in that area from going live until those checks are done. | Register lookups where they exist (NMC or state medical council, Bar Council, ICAI, SEBI) or DigiLocker via API Setu. Add them behind an `IdentityVerificationProvider` so a reviewer sees the register result next to the proof. |
| B4 | **Video calls behind strict networks** | Direct browser-to-browser WebRTC with signalling over Counsel's own WebSocket. Video or voice, a pre-join check, screen share, full screen, consent-based recording, reconnecting after a drop, and a weak-connection notice. Tested between two sessions with stand-in cameras. `ICE_SERVERS` is a setting. | A TURN server, for example coturn on a small public server. Without one, some office and mobile networks will not connect. Counsel's calls stay one-to-one, so no group video server is planned. |
| B5 | **Two-way calendar sync** (Google, Outlook) | Every live booking has a calendar invite file. Availability is managed inside Counsel. | Google Calendar and Microsoft Graph OAuth apps. Then add a `CalendarProvider` that writes each booking to the consultant's calendar and removes busy times from their slots. |
| B6 | **Transcripts and captions** | Recordings are saved to the consultation's files with both people's consent. The recap uses the shared notes. | A speech-to-text service for English, Hindi and Telugu, for example Bhashini, Google Speech-to-Text or Whisper hosted by us. It would produce a transcript of a recording (with consent) and live captions for accessibility. |
| B7 | **AI provider key** | The Claude path for matching and recap summaries is built and off. | An `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, then `AI_PROVIDER=anthropic` in `counsel-service/.env`. |

## Still to build

| T1 | **ForKhatri-wide account deletion** (Counsel's side is done) | Done in Counsel: the You page has "Download my data" (JSON) and "Delete my Counsel data". Deletion is refused while bookings, follow-ups, questions, disputes or unpaid earnings are still open. It removes the profile, evidence, proofs, payout details, private notes, files and messages, and keeps payment and tax records without the name. For the platform there are `GET /internal/v1/members/{id}/deletion-check`, `POST …/erase` and `GET …/export`, which accept only the platform's service key. Still open, on the platform side: ForKhatri has no account-deletion flow that calls modules yet. Vyapar built its own privacy page, so the platform should call each module's erase endpoint when a whole account is deleted. |
| T2 | **Stored-file protection** (done except running a scanner) | Done: recordings are deleted after 90 days and other consultation files after 365 days (changeable in Admin → Settings; notes, action items and recaps are kept). Consultation files, recordings and verification proofs are now encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM (`FILE_ENCRYPTION_KEY`, required outside local development) and decrypted only on an authorised download. Every upload passes through a malware-scan step: `MALWARE_SCANNER=none` by default, or `clamav` to stream each file to a local ClamAV daemon (`CLAMD_HOST`, `CLAMD_PORT`). Infected files are refused, and uploads are refused if scanning is switched on but the scanner is down. Still open: install ClamAV (free, no account needed) on the server and set `MALWARE_SCANNER=clamav`. Profile photos stay public and unencrypted. |
| T3 | **Tax on fees and payouts** (payout details are done) | Done: consultants add a UPI id or a bank account with IFSC on Earnings. The details are encrypted (`PAYOUT_ENCRYPTION_KEY`) and only the last 4 characters are shown. Payouts are refused until a method is added. Still open: GST on the platform fee and TDS on payouts need an accountant's rules. Paying out to these details needs B1. |
| T4 | **A free short message before booking** | Paid written questions now cover "ask before booking". A free, rate-limited "is this the right person?" message is not built. It would need blocking and reporting so it cannot turn into spam. |
| T5 | **Time zones and currencies outside India** | Consultants pick from a short time-zone list, and country options are limited. Prices are in INR only, although each service stores a currency. |
| T7 | **Admin role through the platform** | Admin access comes from the platform's `counsel.admin` scope, or from `ADMIN_MEMBER_IDS` in Counsel's `.env`. The Milavn moderator development account is listed there for testing. Grant `counsel.admin` from the ForKhatri governance console, then remove the development entry. |
| T8 | **Call checks with real devices** | Try one call where someone starts on voice and then turns the camera on. Automated testing saw the video track arrive but could not confirm the picture. Also check full screen on an iPhone, where the call fills the window instead of going true full screen. |
| T9 | **More from Zoom, Meet and Teams** | Done (see "Call polish" above). Still open: live captions, which depend on B6. Picture-in-picture and device switching were checked with stand-in cameras; try them once on a real phone and laptop together with T8. |

## Built from the discovery research (1 Oct 2026)

The owner asked for "near location, sort by ratings etc." and for what helps people find the right consultant and helps consultants stay ahead. Checked against Practo, Zocdoc, GrowthMentor, MentorCruise, Upwork and Fiverr (sources in the report of that date). Everything below uses Counsel's real data; nothing is estimated.

| What | Where it came from | How it works in Counsel |
|---|---|---|
| **Near me** | Zocdoc (distance is a ranking factor), Practo (browse by city), MentorCruise (mentor location filter) | Discover has a place picker: "Anywhere in India", "Use my location" (the browser's position is matched to the nearest listed city on the device; nothing goes to an outside service) or one of 45 listed cities (`app/components/places.py`, names in English, Hindi and Telugu). Cards then show "In your city" or "About 250 km away", a "Within 150 km of …" chip filters, and "Nearest to you" sorts. A short note says plainly that consultations are online and nearness is about local knowledge. The chosen city is remembered on the device. Consultants pick their city from the same list (or type another town, which shows on the profile but has no distance). |
| **Sort options** | Practo (patient stories, experience, fee low/high), Zocdoc (soonest availability) | Best match, Top rated, Most consultations, Soonest available, Price low to high, Price high to low, Newest on Counsel, Most experience, and Nearest (once a city is chosen). **Top rated uses a Bayesian average**: each consultant starts with 5 imaginary reviews at the platform's average, so one 5-star review cannot beat many strong reviews. The same weighted rating now feeds Best match. |
| **More filters** | Practo (Available today, fee ranges), Zocdoc (ratings) | Quick chips: Available now, Free in the next 24 hours, 4★ and above. The filter panel has a price range (min and max), language, minimum rating and verified only; the filter button shows how many are on. |
| **Reply time** | GrowthMentor shows each mentor's average response time and removes unresponsive mentors | "Usually replies within …" on cards and profiles, the median time to answer time requests and paid questions over 180 days, shown only after at least two answers. |
| **Repeat clients share** | Upwork's Job Success Score rests on repeat relationships | The profile shows "Repeat clients (N% came back)" once three or more different people have booked. |
| **How people find you** (consultant dashboard) | Fiverr's impressions → clicks → orders analytics; Upwork's 100% profile rule for badges; Zocdoc's published "how search works" | Last 30 days vs the 30 before: appearances in search, unique profile views (one per viewer per day, stored only as a one-way hash), bookings and views that became bookings. Profile strength (11 items, each one a thing search, filters or seekers use, each linking to where to fix it). Their current place under Best match in their main area, their reply time, a reviews tip, and a plain explanation of what Best match weighs. Tables: `search_impression`, `profile_view` (migration 009). |

## Bookings redesign (1 Oct 2026)

The owner asked for "better bookings, proper cards with them", for the person who booked and for the consultant. Checked against Calendly's meetings page (Upcoming, Pending and Past, grouped by date, details with the invitee's answers), Airbnb's host Today tab (requests with accept/decline and a countdown, "arriving in 9 days"), Airbnb's 24-hour service cancellation rule shown against the start time, Zocdoc (appointments list, cancel from the details page, reminders) and Practo (upcoming and past appointments, video, audio and follow-ups). Everything uses Counsel's real rows; nothing is estimated.

| What | How it works in Counsel |
|---|---|
| **Booking card** (`components/BookingCard.tsx`) | A calendar-style day badge, the service, the other person (photo when the consultant has one), the time range with the reader's time zone ("5:40 – 6:10 am IST"), length, video or voice, and money: "₹1,200 paid" for the person who booked; "You earn ₹1,176 · payout pending / paid out" for the consultant; refunds shown. The consultant also sees the client's brief. Each card offers **one next step** for its state: Pay (with "Pay within 23 hrs 58 mins to keep this time"), Join (when the room opens, with "Starts in 2 mins" or "Happening now"), Mark as completed (consultant, after the end time), Add to calendar (with "Starts in 2 days" and "Full refund if cancelled before …"), Leave a review, View recap, or Book again. Status tags read correctly for each side ("Cancelled by client" vs "Cancelled by you"). |
| **Grouping** (`components/BookingsBoard.tsx`) | A summary strip (Today, Next 7 days, Need your action), Upcoming and Past tabs with counts. Upcoming: **Needs your action** (held times to pay, reviews not written within 30 days, consultations the consultant has not closed), time requests, follow-ups and questions, **Today**, **Coming up**. Past: All / Completed / Cancelled or missed chips and month headings. Someone who both books and consults gets All / I booked / My clients chips. Empty states lead to Discover (or Availability for a consultant). |
| **Consultant bookings** | New **Bookings** tab under Consult (`/provide/bookings`) with the same board for their clients, time requests to answer and questions; the Overview's "Upcoming" now shows the next three as cards. |
| **Booking page** (`app/bookings/[id]`) | A header card with the day badge, status, time with time zone, length and format; the next step right under it (pay with countdown, room open with countdown, "How did it go?" review prompt). **What has happened**: a timeline built only from recorded rows (`GET /bookings/{id}/timeline`): time proposed and accepted, booked, paid, reminders, who joined the room, completed, recap, cancelled, refunds, review, dispute and its decision, payout (consultant only). Money shows the ForKhatri fee, what the consultant receives and the payout state. The cancellation rule that applies right now is highlighted, and under Cancel: "If you cancel now: ₹X back (N%)". Opening `?review=1` opens the review form directly from a card. |
| **Time requests, follow-ups, questions** | Shown as the same cards (day badge, time zone, the reason given, Accept/Decline or Withdraw); follow-ups show sessions used as a bar. |
| **API** | `GET /bookings` takes `role=any|seeker|provider` and returns `provider_photo` and `has_recap`; new `GET /bookings/{id}/timeline`. No migration was needed. |
