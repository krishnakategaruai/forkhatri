# MOD04 Counsel — 02 Functional requirements

**Document status:** 2026-11-22, "as built". Derived from `counsel-service/app/**`, `counsel-web/app/**`, migrations 001-011 and the live schema. IDs: BR (01), FR (here), SCR (03), API (API-REFERENCE.md), TBL (07a), TC (05). Status: built / partial / planned. Tests: none automated exist for Counsel (G-10); all acceptance is manual (05).

## Contents
A Onboarding and verification · B Discovery · C Services and availability · D Booking · E Money · F Follow-ups and questions · G Consultation room · H Reputation and trust · I Notifications · J Safety and admin · K Privacy · L Language, theme, identity

Common rules: every request carries the ForKhatri session cookie; the member is whoever the platform identity service resolves (Counsel never signs anyone in). Errors use `{detail:{code,message}}`. Participants-only checks run in the handler (`participant()`), never only in the UI. Money is in minor units (paise).

## A. Onboarding and verification
**FR-001 Consultant profile (built).** Actors: member. Pre: signed in. Flow: Consult tab -> Profile -> fill headline (max 120), intro (2000), city (from 45 listed or typed), country, time zone, languages, links (linkedin, website, youtube, instagram, portfolio), expertise (max 8, each with years, one primary) -> Save (`PUT /provide/profile`). Rules: first save creates a provider in `draft`; expertise list is replaced wholesale; only active areas accepted; city stored as English label. Data: TBL provider, provider_expertise, audit_log (`provider.created`). Screen SCR-17. BR-01. Accept: reload shows saved values.
**FR-002 Photo (built).** `POST /provide/photo`: jpeg/png/webp up to 5 MB with magic-byte check, stored public under `public/photos/{member}/`, served by `GET /media/photos/...`. Not encrypted (public by design).
**FR-003 Propose a new expertise area (built).** `POST/GET /provide/expertise-requests` -> admin FR-009.
**FR-004 Evidence and private proof (built).** Evidence kinds: experience, qualification, certification, registration, business, portfolio, with title (160), issuer, years, reference number. Proof file image/pdf up to 10 MB, content-sniffed, malware-scanned, AES-GCM encrypted, never on public profile; re-upload resets status to `submitted`. Owner can view and delete; admin view is audited (`evidence.document_viewed`). Endpoints API provide/evidence*. TBL provider_evidence.
**FR-005 Submit for review (built).** Needs headline and at least one expertise (`incomplete` 422). draft or changes_requested -> pending_review; audit `provider.submitted`.
**FR-006 Admin decision (built).** approve | changes_requested | suspend | reinstate with note. suspend pauses all active services; notifies the consultant (`provider.<status>`); audit.
**FR-007 Granular verification (built).** Admin grants/revokes one of 8 dimensions: identity, contact, experience, qualification, certification, registration, business, portfolio; each with note and verifier; audited. Evidence items are decided separately (verified/rejected/submitted). Public profile shows each dimension as its own tag; there is no single "Verified" stamp (one derived badge `verified_professional` exists, see G-07).
**FR-008 Compliance gating (built).** `compliance_rule(area, jurisdiction)` lists required dimensions. Creating or updating a service with status active fails `verification_required` (missing list) until the consultant holds them. Seed rules: legal {identity, qualification, registration} no recording; finance {identity, qualification}; investment IN {identity, qualification, registration} else {identity, qualification}; health {identity, qualification, registration} no recording; mental-health {identity, qualification} no recording. Disclaimer key shown before payment.
**FR-009 Taxonomy management (built).** 26 areas seeded (5 languages). Admin approves a request (slug, 5 names, regulated flag) or upserts/disables areas. Approving also attaches the area to the proposer.

## B. Discovery
**FR-010 Home (built).** `/home`: hero with "Describe it in your own words" box and three example chips, upcoming-count card for signed-in users, area tiles with consultant counts, "People you have consulted", "Trusted people" (8 best match), invite-to-become-consultant card.
**FR-011 Browse by area (built).** Area chips on Discover; areas with zero consultants hidden unless selected.
**FR-012 Search, filters, sort (built).** Free text, area, price min/max (rupees in UI), language, verified only, max duration, minimum rating (3.5/4/4.5), Available now, Free in next 24 h, near a city, within 150 km. Sorts: best, rating (Bayesian, prior 5 reviews at platform mean), consultations, soonest, price asc/desc, newest, experience, nearest (when a city is chosen). Best match score = 0.35 relevance + 0.20 trust (verification count/4) + 0.15 experience (years/15) + 0.10 proven (completed/20) + 0.10 satisfaction + 0.10 reliability. Text relevance is substring token match over name, headline, intro, area names (5 languages), service text. No paid placement. Up to 40 results. "Why" chips: matches_all_terms, well_verified, experienced, proven, reliable. Each appearance logs a search impression (TBL search_impression).
**FR-013 Describe it in your own words (built, rules mode).** `POST /understand` turns up to 600 characters into areas (max 2), duration, budget, up to 6 keywords via keyword hints (English plus some Hindi, Telugu, Marathi, Kannada terms); with `AI_PROVIDER=anthropic` and a key it asks Claude and falls back to rules. Falls back to area-only and first-keyword-only searches if empty. UI shows the understood filters as tags and a Clear link. Known limit: crisis phrases are not routed to helplines (master critique); unauthenticated, unlimited (G-13).
**FR-014 Near me (built).** 45 listed cities with 5-language names; "Use my location" matches the browser position to the nearest listed city on the device (no external geocoder); distance is haversine between centres; chosen city remembered on device (localStorage); note that consultations are online.
**FR-015 Consultant profile page (built).** Header, about, expertise tags, "why trust" (verified dimensions, evidence grouped by kind with "checked" tag, links), services with Book, reviews (name, date), at-a-glance stats, reply time, milestone badges, weekly availability, Save and Report. Non-approved profile visible only to owner and admin.
**FR-016 Saved (built).** Save/unsave from card or profile; list page.
**FR-017 Discovery counters (built).** Impressions per consultant per day; unique profile views per viewer per day via one-way hash (member id or IP+UA); not stored as identities.

## C. Services and availability
**FR-020 Services (built).** Title (100), description (2000), outcomes list, area, duration 15-240, price (> 0, INR), formats (video, audio, screen, files), min notice 0-168 h (default 12), max advance 1-90 days (30), buffer 0-60 min (10), recording allowed, allow custom time, status active/paused/archived. Price changes audited. Archived hidden from the list.
**FR-021 Availability (built).** Weekly rules per weekday: window (start-end, cut on a 15-minute grid) or fixed slot (one exact start). Exceptions per date: day off, blocked partial range, or extra hours, with note. Provider time zone governs; UI shows slots in the viewer's zone.
**FR-022 Slot engine (built).** Slots = rules + exceptions - live bookings with buffer, within min notice and max advance. Booking re-checks `is_free`; a database EXCLUDE constraint on the provider time range (statuses pending_payment, confirmed, in_progress) prevents double booking (`slot_taken` 409).
**FR-023 Available now (built).** Approved consultant goes online 30/60/120 minutes (API accepts 0-240). For services with instant allowed, the next 5-minute mark becomes bookable without notice while online. Discover filter and card badge "Can start now".

## D. Booking
**FR-030 Book and pay (built, sandbox).** Pick day/slot (viewer time zone), optional brief (1500), review price, cancellation tiers and disclaimer, tick agreement, pay. `POST /bookings` snapshots title, duration, price, fee, payable, cancellation policy, recording flag, formats; creates a `payment` row with the adapter reference; `POST /bookings/{id}/pay` verifies and sets confirmed, writes ledger rows (gross, platform_fee, payable), welcome system message, notifies both, audit. Cannot book own service (`own_service`); blocked pairs refused. Unpaid pending bookings expire after 15 minutes (or at `pay_by`).
**FR-031 Propose a time (built).** If a service allows custom time, the seeker proposes date/time (within notice and advance window, no conflict, max 3 pending per consultant). Consultant accepts (booking created held up to 24 h or until start for payment, whichever earlier) or declines with note; seeker can withdraw; past pending requests expire. Nothing charged until accepted and paid.
**FR-032 Cancel (built).** Either participant while pending_payment (-> expired, no refund) or confirmed. Seeker: refund % from snapshot tiers; consultant: `provider_cancel_refund_percent`. Package session: full-refund cancel returns the session. System message, notify other, audit.
**FR-033 Complete (built).** Either participant from start time; sets completed, payout eligible, recap built, review prompt to seeker, payout.eligible to consultant, badges refreshed. Auto: confirmed becomes in_progress at start; in_progress 24 h after end becomes completed and eligible with no join check (G-03).
**FR-034 No-show (built).** Allowed 10 minutes after start by the present party: provider reporting -> `no_show_seeker` (refund 0% by default, payout eligible); seeker reporting -> `no_show_provider` (refund 100%, no payout). Not checked against join events (G-03).
**FR-035 Housekeeping (built).** Every 60 s: expire stale checkouts, auto-progress, send reminders, expire purchases (unanswered questions refund, packages refund unused), dispatch queued deliveries; every 10 min recompute badges; hourly apply retention.
**FR-036 Bookings screens (built).** Combined board: needs-action summary, today/week counts, upcoming and past, time requests, purchases. One "next step" per card: pay, wait for payment, join, mark complete, add to calendar, review, recap, book again.
**FR-037 Timeline (built).** Per booking chronological events from real rows: booked/time requested/accepted, paid, reminders, joined, completed, recap, cancelled, no-show, refunded, reviewed, dispute opened/decided, payout.
**FR-038 Calendar file (built).** `.ics` with join URL and 15-minute alarm; no two-way sync (B5).

## E. Money
**FR-040 Quote (built).** `quote = {gross, platform_fee = round(price x pct/100), processing_fee 0, tax 0, provider_payable = price - fee}`. See G-01 for the owner's additional-fee definition.
**FR-041 Ledger (built).** `ledger_entry` rows per booking or purchase: gross, platform_fee (negative), provider_payable, refund (negative), payout (negative); processing_fee/tax/adjustment kinds exist but are unused.
**FR-042 Payment adapters (built; sandbox only).** `PAYMENT_PROVIDER=mock` (own stand-in, refused unless API bound to loopback) or any name via the shared integrations library (sandbox local only, payment_services = MOD06 future, vendors later). `POST /payments/events` accepts signed `payment.succeeded` and confirms the booking or purchase. No real gateway exists.
**FR-043 Refunds (built).** Booking: percent of gross through the adapter; payment status refunded/partially_refunded; ledger refund; `refund_minor` increases. Admin dispute decision can refund up to what remains.
**FR-044 Payout method (built).** UPI id (`name@bank`) or bank (9-18 digit account + IFSC) with holder name; secret encrypted (Fernet), last4 shown; audited.
**FR-045 Request payout (built, sandbox).** Needs payout method. Sums bookings with payout eligible and completed more than `payout_hold_hours` ago (payable - refund) plus answered questions; one `payout` row marked paid at once; booking `payout_status` paid; ledger payout. `nothing_to_pay` 409 otherwise.
**FR-046 Earnings view (built).** Ready-for-payout amount, method, per-booking breakdown (gross - fee), payouts list; dashboard shows last 30 days, pending, paid.

## F. Follow-ups and paid questions
**FR-050 Follow-ups (built).** Consultant adds 1-3 sessions for a price lower than sessions x service price, valid 30-90 days; offered only to a seeker with a completed consultation with that consultant (booking page "Continue with ...", service page). Buy -> pay once -> each session booked with nothing to pay, carrying its per-session share; cancel 24 h or more ahead gives the session back; buyer can cancel (unused refunded) once no session is booked; expiry refunds unused automatically; consultant paid per completed session. Table `package` + `purchase(kind=package)`.
**FR-051 Paid written questions (built).** Consultant sets price and answer time 1-7 days per service. Seeker asks (10-3000 chars), pays; max 2 open per consultant; consultant answers (10-6000) -> payout eligible; if not answered by `due_at` the full price is refunded automatically. Tables purchase(kind=question), question.

## G. Consultation room
**FR-060 Room access (built).** Opens 15 minutes before start and stays open 2 hours after end for confirmed/in_progress; completed bookings open as a workspace. WebSocket authenticated by single-use ticket (60 s) or cookie; non-participants closed with 4403, unauthenticated 4401. Joining a confirmed booking starts it.
**FR-061 Call (built).** Peer-to-peer WebRTC (one-to-one). Video or voice, pre-join check, camera/mic toggle, screen share, full screen, draggable self-view, picture-in-picture, device pickers that switch mid-call, speaking indicators, reconnect on drop, weak-connection banner (rtt > 0.6 s or loss > 8%) with "switch to voice". Server relays offer/answer/ice/ready/media only; no media on the server. ICE servers from `ICE_SERVERS` (default Google STUN only; no TURN, G-05).
**FR-062 Chat (built).** Per-booking thread (before and after the call), persisted, polled every 8 s on the booking page and live in the room; typing indicator; notification to the other party when not in the room.
**FR-063 Notes (built).** Shared note (live to both, last writer wins with version, 600 ms debounce) and private note per member.
**FR-064 Action items (built).** Add, tick, delete; optional owner; live.
**FR-065 Files (built).** Allow-listed types (images, pdf, text, csv, office, zip, json), size by `MAX_UPLOAD_MB` (25), scanned, encrypted; download audited; uploader can remove; retention 365 days.
**FR-066 Recording (built, consent).** Allowed only if service and area rule allow and both members gave consent (audited); indicator and chime for both; browser MediaRecorder uploads webm (500 MB) as a private file; retention 90 days.
**FR-067 Call feedback (built).** Thumbs plus issues (audio, video, screen, connection, echo, other) per member per booking.
**FR-068 Recap (built).** On completion: shared notes, action items, files compiled into one message and stored (`booking.recap`); AI summary prepended only when Claude is enabled.

## H. Reputation and trust
**FR-070 Review (built).** Seeker only, completed booking, once; overall, expertise, communication, professionalism, punctuality 1-5 plus text (1500). Shown with reviewer's name and date (G-06). Notifies consultant. `hidden` flag exists; no admin screen sets it.
**FR-071 Reputation (built).** Completed count, distinct clients, repeat clients, reliability % (1 - consultant cancels/no-shows over bookings, shown from the first booking, G-07), rating and per-dimension averages, repeat share only from 3 clients, reply time (median of time-request and question replies over 180 days, shown from 2 samples).
**FR-072 Badges and standing (built).** Nine badge families with levels (consultations 1/10/50/100/250/500, clients 10/50/150, repeat_trust 3/10/25, highly_rated 4.8 with 10 reviews, on_time 4.8 with 10 reviews, reliable run 20/50, hours 10/50/100/250, verified_professional, tenure 1/3/5 years). Awards keep the date and numbers at the time. Standing new, rising, established, senior, distinguished. Neither changes price or ranking (D4, D6). Recomputed on completion, review and every 10 minutes.
**FR-073 How you are found (built).** 30 vs previous 30 days: impressions, profile views, bookings, conversion; 11-item profile strength with links; position under Best match in main area; reply time; reviews tip.

## I. Notifications
**FR-080 In-app (built).** Kinds: booking.new/confirmed/cancelled/no_show/reminder_day/reminder_now/recap, review.available/received, payout.eligible, message.new, time_request.new/accepted/declined, package.bought/refunded, question.new/answered/refunded, dispute.decided, badge.earned, provider.approved/changes_requested/suspended, expertise.approved/rejected. Bell shows unread dot; list shows 50, marks all read on open.
**FR-081 Reminders (built).** 24 h and 15 min before start, once each.
**FR-082 Outbound queue (partial).** Channels email/sms/whatsapp/push can be listed in `NOTIFICATION_CHANNELS` (default `inapp,log`); rows are queued in `delivery` and sent every minute by the shared dispatcher, but no contact lookup exists so rows are marked skipped (B2).

## J. Safety and admin
**FR-090 Reports (built).** Any member reports provider/service/booking/message/member with reason; admin marks actioned or dismissed (no automatic effect on the target).
**FR-091 Disputes (built).** Participant opens on a non-pending, non-expired booking (one open at a time); admin decides refund percent capped to the remainder and writes a resolution; both notified.
**FR-092 Blocks (partial).** API to block/unblock; blocks stop booking and questions between the pair; no screen in the web app calls it (unverified: grep of web code shows no use).
**FR-093 Admin console (built).** Overview (queue counts, GTV, fee, refunds, cancellations, no-shows), providers by status and detail (evidence, proofs, verification, services, badge history, audit), expertise requests and taxonomy, reports, disputes, transactions (100), settings (fee %, cancellation tiers and rules, payout hold, retention days; `review_window_days` stored but unused), audit log API. Admin = `counsel.admin` scope or ADMIN_MEMBER_IDS.

## K. Privacy
**FR-100 Export (built).** Download JSON of profile, services, bookings, messages sent, private notes, reviews written, purchases, questions, badges, saved, payout method (masked); audited.
**FR-101 Delete Counsel data (built; SUPERSEDED 2026-11-22 by FR-130..136, it no longer blocks).** Blocked while upcoming bookings, active purchases, open disputes or unpaid earnings exist. Otherwise deletes files from disk, blanks messages and review text and brief, deletes private notes, saved, notifications, deliveries, blocks, time requests, provider evidence/verification/availability/payout method/expertise/counters; services archived, provider suspended; name becomes "Deleted member"; ledger, payment and audit rows stay. Requires typing DELETE.
**FR-102 Retention (built).** Hourly: recordings older than 90 days and other files older than 365 days removed, audited.
**FR-103 Platform internal (built).** `GET .../deletion-check`, `POST .../erase`, `GET .../export` with the platform service key. As of 2026-11-22 identity calls the erase route (FR-131).

## L. Language, theme, identity
**FR-110 Languages (built).** en, hi, te, mr, kn; 1029 keys each; API strings are codes translated on the client; expertise names in 5 languages; language chosen on You page and sent as `X-Counsel-Language`.
**FR-111 Theme (built).** Auto/light/dark stored in `localStorage` key `counsel.theme`.
**FR-120 Sign-in (built, platform-owned).** Signed-out visitors can see public pages; protected pages show a sign-in card linking to the hub with `return_to`; session resolved server-to-server and cached 30 s (positive) / 5 s (negative); optional tunnel handoff code in `proxy.ts`.

## M. Account deletion (added 2026-11-22, built, not browser-checked)
Proven by `counsel-service/scripts/check_account_deletion.py` (16 checks pass). All 40 tables reviewed (37 acted on, 3 catalogue tables hold no member data). No new tables or settings.

**FR-130 Delete my data in Counsel (kind B).** Flow: You page, "Your data", "Delete my data in Counsel", dialog lists warnings (`GET /me/deletion-check`), member types DELETE, `POST /me/delete-account-data` `{"confirm":"DELETE"}`, member is sent to the ForKhatri entrance. Rules: typed DELETE; re-authentication is the existing ForKhatri session; 3 attempts per hour per member; idempotent; the ForKhatri account is untouched. The older `POST /me/delete` is kept and does the same. Acceptance: member rows gone or tombstoned, files removed, other parties notified, second call returns `nothing_to_delete`.
**FR-131 Erase on behalf of the platform (kind A).** `POST /internal/v1/members/{member_id}/erase`, service key plus header `X-ForKhatri-Service: identity` (exactly that name). Returns `{module, status: done | nothing_to_delete, deleted{}, retained[], files_removed}`. Called by identity with hub-side retry (30 s doubling to 6 h, 7 days, admin "stuck deletions" list).
**FR-132 One transaction.** `privacy.erase_member` runs everything in one transaction as `counsel_app`; files are removed after commit; session, name and call-ticket caches dropped. Idempotent through the `erased_member` receipt.
**FR-133 Close open work first.** Future bookings (consumer or expert) cancelled by the normal cancel routine with refund row and ledger entry where a payment exists; expert side full refund; under-way consultations refunded (expert) or counted as held (consumer); open packages and written questions refunded and closed; pending time requests deleted. Notices: `booking.cancelled_deleted`, `purchase.closed_deleted`.
**FR-134 Stand-in member.** Retained rows point at `sha256("counsel-erased:" + member_id + service key)`, shown as "Deleted member". Completed consultations survive for the other party; reviews about a deleted expert are deleted; ratings the member gave stay without name or text.
**FR-135 Warnings, not blockers.** `GET /me/deletion-check` and the internal deletion-check no longer refuse; `blockers` are shown as "This will also happen right now". Supersedes the blocking in FR-101.
**FR-136 Export extended.** `GET /me/export` covers the full inventory (profile, services, packages, availability, file metadata, notifications, blocks, disputes, reports and more).
Hub-side requirements for kind A are in `docs/ParentApp/ACCOUNT-DELETION.md`.
